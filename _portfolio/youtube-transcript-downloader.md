Download the full transcript of any public YouTube video in a second or two. Single transcripts are free, with no sign-up required.

## Features
- **Transcript download:** paste a YouTube link and copy or export the transcript in 9 formats (TXT, SRT, VTT, JSON, CSV, Markdown, and ready-to-use formats for ChatGPT, Claude and NotebookLM).
- **AI Summary:** short, medium or in-depth summaries generated from the full transcript.
- **AI Chapters:** timestamped chapter lists anchored to the real topic breaks, ready to paste into a video description.
- **AI Polish:** cleans up auto-generated captions by restoring punctuation, removing filler words and fixing misheard names, while keeping every word that was said.
- **Bulk download:** transcripts from entire channels or playlists in a single ZIP.
- **Developer API:** REST API with bearer token auth and single and bulk endpoints.
